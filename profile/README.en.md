### How you stay in control

1. **Before development:** project discovery, NDA, agreed scope, architecture, and an estimate.
2. **During delivery:** a dedicated team, direct access to engineers, and weekly demonstrations.
3. **At launch:** deployment, source code, documentation, and knowledge transfer to your team.
4. **After launch:** support on agreed terms.
